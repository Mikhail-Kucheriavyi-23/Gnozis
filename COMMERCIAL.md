# Gnozis Commercial Model

Gnozis connects opportunities, contracts, verified deliverables and commercial settlement.

## Commercial chain

```
Opportunity
  ↓
Contract
  ↓
Deliverable
  ↓
Verification
  ↓
Acceptance
  ↓
Settlement
  ↓
License / IP
```

## Important separation

A repository or commit does not determine ownership of intellectual property by itself. Contractual terms and applicable licenses govern the relevant rights.

Payment credentials and financial secrets do not belong in Gnozis repositories.

## Milestones

Commercial contracts may use milestones tied to explicit deliverables and acceptance criteria.

## Public vs private

Public surfaces may expose opportunity and high-level commercial information. Negotiated rates, confidential financial information and proprietary Kernel technology remain controlled.

Settlement state may reference an external payment system without storing its secrets inside Gnozis.

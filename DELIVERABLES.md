# Gnozis Deliverables & Evidence

Gnozis separates work output from its verification and acceptance.

## Chain

```
Requirement
  ↓
Deliverable
  ↓
Artifact
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Verification
  ↓
Acceptance
  ↓
Result
```

A Git commit, document, dataset, build or research record can be an artifact. It is not automatically an accepted deliverable.

## Acceptance

Acceptance is tied to explicit criteria and evidence.

Possible states include:

- PENDING
- PASSED
- FAILED
- PARTIAL
- DISPUTED
- WAIVED

## Integrity

Important artifacts can be identified by immutable revision and content digest. A changed artifact may require a new verification cycle.

This creates a traceable connection between contracts, project work and verifiable results.

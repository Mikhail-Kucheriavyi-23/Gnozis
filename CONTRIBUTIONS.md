# Gnozis Contributions

Gnozis can represent contribution history as verifiable records rather than universal ratings.

## Contribution graph

```
Person / Organization
  ↓
Role
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Contract
  ↓
Deliverable
  ↓
Artifact
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Verification
  ↓
Acceptance
  ↓
Result
```

## What this means

A participant can demonstrate what they actually contributed, which project it belonged to, which artifact resulted, and whether the defined work was accepted.

The base system does not assign a universal star rating or ranking.

## Visibility

Contribution records can have scoped visibility depending on privacy, contract and licensing requirements.

Historical records should remain traceable when appropriate, even after a role or capability ends.

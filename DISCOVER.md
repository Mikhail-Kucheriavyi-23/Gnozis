# Discover Gnozis

Gnozis is designed as an open discovery surface for knowledge, research and opportunities.

## Discover

- Knowledge
- Research Questions
- Open Problems
- Capabilities
- Projects
- Partnerships
- Contract Opportunities
- Products and Prototypes
- Investment Opportunities

## From discovery to work

```
Discovery
  ↓
Interest
  ↓
Proposal
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Contract
  ↓
Workspace
  ↓
Deliverable
  ↓
Acceptance
```

Public discovery does not grant private access.

Evidence and provenance should remain visible where appropriate, while confidential commercial information and proprietary Kernel internals remain controlled.

Gnozis does not require a universal ranking of people or opportunities. Users can evaluate the documented facts relevant to their own goals.

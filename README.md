# GNOSIS

## Autopoietic Systems for Scientific Discovery & Intelligence

### Universal Computational Research Platform

### UROBOROS — Ψ-Core v33

> **A general computational architecture for autopoietic systems, modeling, reasoning, evolution, scientific research and discovery.**

GNOSIS is an open research platform built around **UROBOROS — Ψ-Core v33**, a recursive computational core designed to investigate **autopoietic systems**: systems capable of maintaining and transforming their organization through internally connected processes.

The architecture provides a general framework for representing:

* states;
* relations;
* dynamics;
* memory;
* internal models;
* alternative trajectories;
* evaluation;
* selection;
* recursive evolution.

Its central research objective is to investigate whether a relatively minimal computational architecture can provide a common foundation for **self-maintaining, adaptive and evolving systems** across different scientific and technological domains.

> **Model. Reason. Evolve. Test. Discover.**

# What Is an Autopoietic System?

In the context of GNOSIS, an **autopoietic system** is a system whose organization is maintained and transformed through processes internal to the system itself.

Conceptually:

$$
\boxed{
System
\rightarrow
Internal\ Processes
\rightarrow
Transformation
\rightarrow
New\ System\ State
\rightarrow
Internal\ Processes
}
$$

The term is used here as a **computational systems concept**.

GNOSIS does not claim that UROBOROS is biological life.

Instead, it provides a computational framework for investigating architectures with properties such as:

* self-maintenance;
* internal state transformation;
* recursive organization;
* adaptation;
* persistence;
* self-modeling;
* generation of alternatives;
* evaluation and selection;
* continuous evolution.

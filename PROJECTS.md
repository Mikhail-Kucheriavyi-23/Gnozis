# Gnozis Projects & Workspaces

A **Project** is the bounded unit of coordinated work.

A **Workspace** is the scoped environment connecting the people, repositories, tasks, contracts and artifacts required by that project.

## Typical flow

```
Opportunity
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Workspace
  ├── People
  ├── Tasks
  ├── Repositories
  ├── Research
  ├── Contracts
  └── Deliverables
  ↓
Evidence / Acceptance
  ↓
Result
```

Projects may use multiple repositories without turning repository membership into contractual authority.

Workspace access is scoped by identity, role, capability, project and contract.

The private Kernel remains outside the normal project workspace. Kernel influence requires its own authorization and verification boundaries.

# Research Machine Contract Interface

## Role

The older Gnozis repository is the research-side Research Machine. It preserves mathematical, philosophical, experimental and external-world research with provenance.

It is not a second Core and it does not authorize Gnozis-V2 mutation.

## Permanent interface

Research Machine
→ Mathematical Reference
→ Reconciliation
→ Engineering consequence
→ Gnozis-V2 bounded task
→ Runtime/CI evidence
→ Audit
→ revised research reference

## Mathematical namespace

The mathematical reference is under:
research_machine/mathematics/

It is maintained independently of Python implementation architecture.

## Required status discipline

Research claims use:
HYPOTHESIS | SUPPORTED | CONTRADICTED | UNDETERMINED | INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE | OUT_OF_SCOPE

Mathematical objects additionally use:
PROVEN | DERIVED | SUPPORTED | HYPOTHESIS | COUNTEREXAMPLE | CONTRADICTED | OPEN | UNPROVEN

## Non-authority rule

Research findings, mathematical claims, stored artifacts and lineage do not directly mutate canonical Ψ-Core.

All engineering consequences require explicit translation, bounded implementation, verification and governance.
